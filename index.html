Brief

I am a graduate student at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences of the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (ICTS-TIFR). My research interests lie at the interface of many-body quantum dynamics and quantum information. A complete list of my publications is available on my Google Scholar profile.

Before joining ICTS-TIFR in 2023, I was a Bachelor's Student at Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee. I grew up in Sundernagar, Himachal Pradesh, where I also completed my schooling in 2019.

Research

I study locally interacting quantum many-body systems out of equilibrium, and the universal features they exhibit in the scrambling of quantum information. My current work focuses on:

  • Quantum chaos beyond random-matrix theory. Correlations in locally interacting systems that go beyond the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis, and how they shape the spatiotemporal profile of information scrambling and entanglement growth.
  • Thermalization, localization, and integrability. Information-theoretic characterizations of these dynamical regimes.
  • Nonequilibrium phases and phase transitions. Measurement-induced phase transitions and their critical properties; phase transitions in nonstabilizerness (“magic”).
  • Hydrodynamics and quantum information. How hydrodynamic modes in systems with local conservation laws affect the dynamics of quantum information, entanglement, and other quantum resources.
